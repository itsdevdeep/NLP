Exams In NetMath

* All exams will be held online.
* All exams have scheduled exam dates. Any conflicts with posted exam dates
must be cleared with instructors immediately upon registration.
* Courses may have two or three midterm exams and one final exam.
* Final exams for Summer Session courses will be held on August 8, 2025, unless
your course states otherwise.

* Math 112, 114, and 115 (ALEKS-based courses) will use Respondus for the
proctored final exam.
* Please refer to your course for more information.

Necessary Materials
(for all exams)

* Computer with internet
connection.
* Blank sheets of paper
(approximately 15 sheets) with
your name, course number,
and page number printed
across the top of each
page.
* Writing utensils (preferably
black or blue pen, but dark
pencils are also acceptable).
* Phone/Mobile Device capable of
capturing images/scanning
documents; or a scanner with
email capabilities.
* Photo Identification Card
(acceptable IDs include driver's
license, school ID, state ID card,
passport, etc.; no U.S. Military ID
cards can be used due to
photocopy restrictions).
* Please note: You will not need a
calculator. The use of calculators is
not permitted while taking
NetMath exams.

[[page 9]]
Taking Your Midterms (241, 347, 416, 417,
441 & 444)

* On the date of your midterm,
you will log into your course at:
https://canvas.illinois.edu/
* Exams begin at either 10am
CDT or 9pm CDT. Once you
open the exam, you must
complete it.
* To access the exam, select the
link on your Canvas course
page and follow the
instructions to connect to
Record+.
* Your exam will be a viewable
PDF. You will not print or
download your exam.
* Write your solutions on the
blank sheets of paper. Only
write on one side of each page.
* Once you have completed your
exam or time has ended, you
will need to upload your
solutions to Gradescope.
* Using your mobile phone,
create a PDF of your exam
solutions.
* Upload instructions will be
provided in your course.

[[page 10]]
Final Scheduling with ProctorU (241, 347,
416, 417, 441 & 444)
* Final exams for all courses will be held on Friday, August 8, 2025, unless your
course states otherwise.
- If your course uses ProctorU for the final: To secure your slot, follow the
ProctorU invitation that will be emailed to you.
- Schedule your reservation within the timeframe of your course
- Be sure to select the exam SU25
* A few minutes before the start of your exam, log into the ProctorU page for
your exam to check-in with your proctor.
* Your proctor will then direct you to your student dashboard, and they will enter
a password.

[[page 11]]
Exam Submissions & Grading
* All exams are timed. Late submissions risk a grading penalty up to and
including a grade of zero.
* If you encounter technical difficulties during your exam, you must contact
the NetMath office as soon as possible, during the exam taking window,
at
netmath@illinois.edu

[[page 12]]
Academic Accommodations
* Contact NetMath as soon as possible regarding academic
accommodations.
- Supporting documentation will be required
* Accommodation questions should be directed to:
instruction@netmath.illinois.edu
* Due to the 8-week timeframe, it is crucial for students to contact
NetMath as quickly as possible to ensure accommodations are in place
before starting a summer semester course.
